## Key Learnings

1. **Warm intros convert at 3× the rate of cold emails.** Two of our five positive responses came via warm intros. We need to prioritize getting introductions from existing network.

2. **Hardware investors respond best to concrete metrics.** "X simulations run, Y users, Z% accuracy correlation" got better responses than market size arguments.

3. **Grants are slow but promising.** NSF SBIR is a strong fit and doesn't dilute equity. Allocation of 2 weeks/month to grant writing is worthwhile.

4. **Micro-funds are more responsive than VCs** at this stage. VCs want $10k+ MRR or a clear path there. Micro-funds are willing to bet on the team and the thesis.

5. **Teaching people to say yes.** Follow-up templates (Day 91, 92) need to make it trivially easy for an investor to take the next step. Reducing friction in scheduling and information sharing.
