## 2-Minute Elevator Pitch

> "Every antenna engineer eventually hits the same wall. To simulate an antenna accurately, you need a tool like HFSS or CST — $15,000 a year, tied to a specific workstation, with a licensing model designed for Fortune 500 procurement departments.
>
> Open-source alternatives are free, but they're also fragile, CLI-only, and require you to debug the solver before you debug your design.
>
> AntennaForge is what fits in the middle. It's a cloud-native, browser-based antenna simulation platform. You open a tab, you draw your antenna, you hit simulate. Our GPU-accelerated FDTD solver runs in the cloud and returns S-parameters, radiation patterns, and efficiency plots in minutes — accurate within 5% of measured results.
>
> For consultants, it's $79/month and you never turn down a contract. For startups, it's the tool that gets your IoT product to market without a $20k tooling line item. For universities, it's what lets every student graduate having run real simulations.
>
> We're launching in Q3. The beta opens next month."
