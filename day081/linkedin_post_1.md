I spent $30k on antenna simulation software last year. Here's why I'm never doing that again.

Three years ago, I was an RF engineer at a IoT hardware startup. We needed to design a custom antenna for a smart sensor. So we did what every RF team does — we bought a traditional EM simulation suite.

One license: $18,000.
Annual maintenance: $3,600.
Dedicated workstation with enough RAM to run it: $5,000.
IT setup, license server management, VPN for remote work: priceless (in the worst way).

Total first-year cost: roughly $30,000.

And here's the thing — most of the time, the software sat idle. We'd run a simulation, wait 45 minutes, tweak a parameter, wait another 45 minutes. The workstation was a paperweight the rest of the day.

Worse: we were locked in. Want to collaborate with a contract engineer? They need a license. Need to run a quick simulation from home? VPN into the license server, hope it's not checked out. Consultant asks for your project file? They can't open it without a $20k seat.

That model made sense in 2005. In 2025, it's absurd.

So when we started AntennaForge, we asked: what if the solver runs in the cloud? What if you pay per simulation instead of per seat? What if you can open your browser on any machine — a Chromebook, a borrowed laptop, your phone — and run a full 3D EM simulation?

That's what we built.

No install. No license key. No workstation lock-in. Your projects are in the cloud, accessible from anywhere, shareable with anyone.

The RF industry deserves modern tools. We're building them.
