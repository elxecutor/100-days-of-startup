# Day 76 — AntennaForge Sales Funnel

## Funnel Overview

```
Awareness → Interest → Evaluation → Trial → Paid → Retained → Referral → Upsell
```

---

## Stage Details

### 1. Awareness
| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Customer Mindset** | "I need a better/faster/cheaper way to simulate antennas." |
| **Our Action** | Content marketing (blog, LinkedIn, Twitter), SEO, community presence (Reddit r/rfelectronics, Hackaday) |
| **Channel** | Organic social, Google Search, engineering forums, word-of-mouth |
| **Metric** | Unique visitors to landing page |
| **Target Rate** | 5,000 unique visitors/mo by month 6 |

### 2. Interest
| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Customer Mindset** | "This looks promising. What does it actually do?" |
| **Our Action** | Feature demo video on landing page, comparison page, use-case gallery |
| **Channel** | Landing page, blog, YouTube demo |
| **Metric** | Landing page → "Start Free Trial" click rate |
| **Target Rate** | 20% (1,000 visitors → 200 free trial starts) |

### 3. Evaluation
| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Customer Mindset** | "Can this handle my specific antenna design?" |
| **Our Action** | Onboarding email sequence (3 emails: welcome, first sim tutorial, advanced tips), in-app guidance |
| **Channel** | Email (ConvertKit), in-app tooltips |
| **Metric** | Signup → First simulation completed |
| **Target Rate** | 60% (200 signups → 120 first sims) |

### 4. Trial
| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Customer Mindset** | "I want to see real results before paying." |
| **Our Action** | 14-day full-feature trial, daily usage tips email, check-in from CEO at day 7, "Your trial expires" email at day 12 |
| **Channel** | In-app, email, Crisp live chat |
| **Metric** | Trial → Paid conversion rate |
| **Target Rate** | 25% (120 trials → 30 paid) |

### 5. Paid
| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Customer Mindset** | "This is worth paying for. Which plan fits?" |
| **Our Action** | Pricing page with comparison, plan selector quiz, annual discount offer |
| **Channel** | In-app upgrade flow, email |
| **Metric** | Conversion rate, ACV (average contract value) |
| **Target Rate** | 25% conversion; $249 avg monthly revenue per customer |

### 6. Retained
| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Customer Mindset** | "This tool is part of my workflow now." |
| **Our Action** | Monthly product updates email, feature request board, usage stats dashboard, proactive support outreach |
| **Channel** | Email, in-app notifications, Crisp |
| **Metric** | Monthly churn rate |
| **Target Rate** | <5% monthly churn (≥95% retention) |

### 7. Referral
| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Customer Mindset** | "My colleagues/peers should use this too." |
| **Our Action** | Referral program: 1 month free per successful referral, shareable referral link in dashboard |
| **Channel** | In-app referral widget, email |
| **Metric** | Referral rate (referrals / active customers) |
| **Target Rate** | 10% of customers refer ≥1 new customer per quarter |

### 8. Upsell
| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Customer Mindset** | "I need more simulations, more seats, or team features." |
| **Our Action** | Usage threshold alerts ("You've used 80% of your sims"), plan comparison in billing, team invite prompt |
| **Channel** | In-app notification, email |
| **Metric** | Upgrade rate (monthly) |
| **Target Rate** | 5% of customers upgrade to higher tier per month |

## Tactics by Stage

**Awareness:** Guest posts on EE blogs, "Antenna Design Weekly" newsletter curation, Stack Exchange answers with backlinks.

**Interest:** Social proof banner ("Join 200+ RF engineers"), live demo booking button, ROI calculator widget.

**Evaluation:** "First simulation in 5 minutes" template library, personalized onboarding call for teams, Slack community access.

**Trial:** Day 3 email with power-user tips, day 7 personal check-in, day 12 "your designs expire soon" reminder if unused.

**Paid:** Seamless credit card flow, 30-day money-back guarantee, annual plan upsell at 20% off.

**Retained:** Monthly "what we shipped" email, usage benchmark report, customer health scoring (sims/active days).

**Referral:** Double-sided incentive (referrer gets 1 month free, referee gets 50% off first month).

**Upsell:** Automated email when hitting 80% of plan sim limit, team invite upsell modal on collaboration page.
